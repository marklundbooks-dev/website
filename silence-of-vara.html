The Silence of Vara

Book One

For fifteen years, high priestess Vara has been the one thing a fractured empire feared — because the gods spoke through her, and her prophecies ended massacres and held a fragile peace together. Then the voices stopped. Three years into a silence no one else knows about, Vara is governing on memory, instinct, and the terrifying question of whether she was ever truly chosen at all — when a stranger arrives from the ruins of the war, claiming the gods have chosen him instead.
