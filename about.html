About

A.M. Marklund

A.M. Marklund is a fantasy author with roots in Sweden. When not writing, she spends her time at her 9-5, or training for a triathalon. Out in the wild you will never see her turn down a good book, a board game, or an excuse to talk about fantasy lore.

She is passionate about writing character-driven fantasy about identity, truth, and impossible choices. Her stories explore what happens when the roles, beliefs, and systems that shape us begin to unravel.
